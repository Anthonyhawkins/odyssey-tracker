Your quest guide

Main quest
The overall goal of your journey. Its thicker central line connects the objectives that move it forward.
!Side quest
A detour from the current branch: an approval, dependency, or unexpected issue. It runs independently and can rejoin its parent branch.
.Objective
A step toward a quest’s goal. Adding an objective continues the same branch.
?Completion
Marks a card’s work as done. Rejoin parent records a separate merge; completing a card does not complete its children.
×Abandonment
Terminates a questline while retaining its work history, reason, and date. It closes the branch without completing or merging it.
+Parallel objective
Use + beside any open questline node to create a group, including on side quests. Parallel work shares a subtle grid container, up to three rows deep, with independent paths and the same questline color.
